02

Plan — from the board down to the last subtask

Every project has a board, list, timeline, calendar and activity feed. A task carries its assignee, dates, milestone, effort, dependencies and subtasks, and an audit issue from the field opens a task here by itself.

  • Kanban board with drag and drop between stages
  • A task drawer for progress, effort, dependencies, subtasks and attachments
  • Closing an audit task writes Completed back to the client's own system

03

Track — timelines and reports that show the truth

The timeline lays every project against today, with who is in charge and where nobody is. Reports turns the same data into status, trend and delivery charts for the weekly review.

  • Day, week and month timeline with stall markers
  • Tasks by status, completion trend and priority breakdown
  • Built on volt-svelte, the team’s own Svelte 5 component kit

04

Survey — every pack, measured on a phone

IR Ops rebuilt the legacy product survey for a merchandiser's phone. Find the product, scan or key in its barcode, type its width and height, and a 3D pack re-scales to match — the same pack that has been floating over these screens.

  • Camera barcode reader for 21 formats, on self-hosted WebAssembly
  • A three.js pack that re-scales as sizes are typed, with the product photo to check against
  • Category tabs, search and an Incomplete / Completed split across the whole catalogue
